About the Program

The NUBTK CS Program is a non-profit initiative designed to help students in both academic and non-academic areas. It focuses on enhancing problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and understanding of future technologies. The program introduces students to various fields of computer science and guides them on how to begin developing their skills effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the NUBTK CS Program? ▼
The program is open to all students interested in computer science, regardless of their current skill level. Whether you're a complete beginner or have some programming experience, our curriculum is designed to support your learning journey.
What is the program duration? ▼
The NUBTK Fall CS25 program runs for six months, providing comprehensive coverage of computer science fundamentals and advanced topics through structured phases.
Is there any fee to join the program? ▼
No, the NUBTK CS Program is a non-profit initiative and completely free for all students. We believe in making quality computer science education accessible to everyone.

Download the syllabus for detailed information.
How can I register for the program? ▼
