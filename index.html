About the Program

The NUBTK CS Program is a non-profit initiative designed to help students in both academic and non-academic areas. It focuses on enhancing problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and understanding of future technologies. The program introduces students to various fields of computer science and guides them on how to begin developing their skills effectively.
